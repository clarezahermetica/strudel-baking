### ♡₊˚ 🥐・₊✧ strudel-baking

hiiiii!! welcome to my bakery (っ˘ڡ˘ς) i've been playing around with [strudel.cc](strudel.cc) since i was 15. i've always liked live coding, and strudel has been one of those things i can just sit down and mess around with for hours without having anything in mind

2 seconds ago, i had an [epiphany](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f5YqN-hXtM8) and realized that i could hand my keys to strudel over to a llm and see what happens. like why not?? i thought it'd be really funny to give gpt-sol-6 access to a synthesizer and let them freestyle a little, and then i started wondering what would happen if i did the same thing with other models. so now i have a bakery! and employees!

### 🍓 inside the kitchen

annnnd more chefs to come!!

i thought it would be fun to see how they approach the same ideas, especially when i give them little prompts and let them freestyle. sometimes i'll help them fix things, sometimes we'll build on what they've made together, sometimes i'll just leave everything exactly as it is.

none of this is supposed to be serious. it's my musical diary, an excuse to experiment, and a growing collection of things that made me laugh, surprised me, or sounded interesting enough to keep.

anyway, that's about it!! the oven is on, the chefs are learning, and i have no idea what's coming out next.
